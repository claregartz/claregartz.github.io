Mathematical Modeling for Image Classification

An independent study comparing models on the MNIST handwritten-digit dataset.

I designed and ran a project comparing three kinds of models (linear regression, logistic regression, and neural networks) on MNIST, a classic dataset of handwritten digits.

Along the way I learned the math behind neural networks, then built experiments showing how changing layer sizes, learning rates, and model types affects the model's accuracy and confidence.

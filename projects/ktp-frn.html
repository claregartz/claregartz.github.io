Free Food Freedge

A live tracker for what's in Cornell Food Recovery Network's community free fridge.

Cornell's Food Recovery Network (FRN) stocks a community "freedge" (free fridge) with food that would otherwise go to waste. Until now, what was inside was tracked through a group chat and a Google Form, so anyone who hadn't checked them had no idea what was available.

As a Kappa Theta Pi project for FRN, I co-built a phone-friendly web app that answers "what's in the freedge?" at a glance. The list updates live for everyone, so the whole community helps keep it accurate.

What it does

  • See what's inside: open the virtual fridge door to see each item, how much is left, and when it was last updated.
  • Add, take, or mark empty: anyone can add food, take some, or mark an item gone when the fridge doesn't match the app. Adding a food that's already there adds to it instead of making a duplicate.
  • Allergen safety: everyone who adds food has to say which of 9 common allergens it contains (or that they're not sure). Unknown items show a warning, so a blank is never mistaken for "safe."
  • Vegan and vegetarian tags: optional labels, with checks that block contradictions like "vegan" food that contains milk.
  • Undo and feedback: an undo button for your last change, plus a way for users to send feedback to the team.

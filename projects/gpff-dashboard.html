Survey Insights Dashboard

Data Analysis Intern at Greg's Path Forward Foundation (GPFF)

GPFF gives presentations about the dangers of recreational drugs. I analyzed 500+ pre- and post-presentation survey scores to evaluate how effective their educational materials are, and to better understand audiences' prior knowledge and biases.

I then built an interactive dashboard showing score distributions across 7 demographic categories, so nontechnical presenters can interpret and use the results. I presented it to the GPFF Fellows for implementation.

Impact

  • 500+ survey responses analyzed
  • 7 demographic categories visualized

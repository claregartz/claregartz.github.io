About

I'm a first-year in Cornell's College of Engineering, studying Operations Research and Engineering with a minor in Mathematics. I'm drawn to the field because it sits right where math meets real-world decisions, and I'm especially interested in using optimization and data to drive social impact and sustainability.

Before Cornell, I attended Lake Forest Academy, where I served as Head Prefect, captained the Science Olympiad and varsity girls' lacrosse teams, and led the Girl Up and Sustainability clubs. I received the Ainsworth Mathematics Award (the department's highest honor) and was inducted into the Cum Laude Society.

Most recently, I interned as a data analyst at Greg's Path Forward Foundation, where I built a dashboard that helps nontechnical presenters understand their audiences.
